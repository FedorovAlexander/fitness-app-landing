Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our follow-along workout app

What is Follow Along Workouts?

Follow Along Workouts is a free fitness app that curates 5,000+ YouTube follow-along workout videos into one ad-free experience. Browse by difficulty, duration, and muscle group, follow structured home workout plans, save favorites, and track your progress.

Is Follow Along Workouts free?

Yes. The app is free to download and includes all videos, ad-free playback, advanced filters, and community access. Premium features — saved workouts, workout history, detailed statistics, and custom workout plans — are available for $2.50 per month.

How is this different from watching workouts on YouTube?

YouTube is full of ads, shorts, and distractions. Follow Along Workouts gives you a focused fitness experience with curated videos, structured multi-week plans, better search filters, and progress tracking — so you spend less time scrolling and more time training.

Are there workout plans for beginners?

Yes. Ready-made workout plans are built from the best curated YouTube videos. Beginners can pick a plan and start in three clicks without planning each session. Customize duration, training days per week, and intensity to match your schedule.

Is the app available on iOS and Android?

Yes. Download Follow Along Workouts for free on the Apple App Store or Google Play.
